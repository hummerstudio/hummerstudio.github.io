It covers: import, create, build, distribute, load

![Asset workflow in Unity](https://docs.unity.cn/cn/2020.3/uploads/Main/AssetWorkflowOverview.svg)

The image above shows the typical workflow for using assets in Unity. Each column represents a separate step, described below:

**Import** assets into the Unity Editor

Use the Unity Editor to **create** content from these assets.

**Build** your application or game files, and optionally the accompanying content packs

**Distribute** the built files so that your users can reach them through a publisher or an app store

**Load** further updates at runtime as needed, based on your users' behavior and on how you group and bundle your content.
