Innocence is lovely;

Innocence is terrifying.

Reality is beautiful;

Reality is cruel.

<!--以上为摘要内容-->

---

The two sides of a contradiction are one, hard to separate,

a seesaw in dynamic balance,

as one end rises the other end falls,

as one place fades another place grows.

---

The truly balanced state is very brief,

lasting only an instant.

More often it is

that as one finishes singing the next takes the stage,

and there is always a leader.

---

Balance is in fact not level;

more precisely it is stability,

a swaying within an acceptable range.
