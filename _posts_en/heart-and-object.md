When the heart is one, there is faith and joy;

When things are set apart, there is doubt and sorrow.

The human heart loves stillness, and returns;

<!--以上为摘要内容-->

Desire for things never stops, and moves forward.

When desire for things pulls at the heart, stillness cannot be had;

When the heart sends desire away, the heart is still and the spirit clear.
