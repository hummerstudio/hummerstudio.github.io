A directional pad (D-pad, short for `directional pad`) is a set of buttons arranged in an arrow or cross design that provides commands in four directions - up, down, left and right, or north, south, east and west.

This type of physical interface control is very popular in gaming devices and other uses, such as game controllers and TV remotes.
